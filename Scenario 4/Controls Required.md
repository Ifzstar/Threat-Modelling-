**Controls Required:**
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA): Require users to authenticate with an additional factor (e.g., SMS code, authentication app, or biometrics) to prevent access even if credentials are compromised.

Advanced Phishing Detection: Deploy AI-powered email and SMS filtering systems to detect and block phishing attempts before they reach users.

Credential Monitoring: Continuously monitor for compromised credentials on the dark web or other forums to take preventive action.

Behavioral Analytics: Use machine learning to track unusual login behavior (e.g., unusual locations, devices, or login times) to flag potential breaches.

Rate Limiting:Limit the number of login attempts from a single IP address or within a specific time frame to prevent automated credential validation.

Captcha Implementation: Add CAPTCHA challenges on login portals to block bots from submitting forms automatically.

Encrypted Data Transmission: Ensure TLS/SSL is used to encrypt data in transit, protecting credentials from interception during phishing attacks.

Awareness Training: Educate users about phishing attacks, including how to identify suspicious links and report them promptly.

Domain-Based Message Authentication, Reporting, and Conformance (DMARC): Implement DMARC, DKIM, and SPF to prevent spoofing of organizational domains for phishing campaigns.

Credential Access Policies: Enforce strict password policies (e.g., complexity, expiration) and disallow reuse of old or weak passwords.

Zero Trust Architecture: Adopt a zero-trust approach requiring continuous validation of users and devices, even after initial authentication.
